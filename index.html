04 — Rules & FAQ

Before you sign up

  1. Acts run to a five-minute maximum. The stage manager will hold you to it.
  2. One entry per student. You may appear in someone else's group act as well.
  3. Backing tracks as MP3 or WAV, emailed by 4 December, named with your reference number.
  4. Attendance at the technical rehearsal on 9 December is compulsory.
  5. Nothing that breaks university conduct rules — the panel's decision is final.
Can we enter as a group?

Yes — up to twelve performers on stage. Register once, under whoever will be the contact, and set the number of performers.

What if my category is full?

The card turns amber when a category is nearly full and closes when it's out of slots. Entries after that go on the waiting list, and the office contacts you if a slot frees up.

Do I need my own instrument?

A piano, drum kit and two guitar amps are on stage. Bring anything else yourself, and list it under technical needs.

Are there prizes?

First place takes the Golden Star and a place at the inter-university festival in June. Second and third receive campus vouchers.
